# SSH MountMate v0.6.20

- Restore direct quota commands for OpenSSH and interactive transports instead of the `operations/about` path introduced in v0.6.19. Native SFTP retains complete block/inode soft-quota reporting through its authenticated mount session.
- Keep terminal-only SSH settings out of the SFTP stream: mounts disable PTY allocation, `LocalCommand` and `RemoteCommand`, while preserving the configured server, identity, jump host and host-key policy.
- Stop reporting rclone's missing native host-key validation as a warning when validation belongs to external OpenSSH. Preserve bounded OpenSSH startup diagnostics when SFTP initialization fails.
- Explain `packet too long` at SFTP startup as an invalid protocol stream, not a quota or file-size limit. Server-specific failures still require the resulting SSH diagnostics to identify their cause.
- Remount after upgrading to use the new connector options and bundled rclone.

---

- 恢复 OpenSSH 和交互式连接原来的独立配额命令，撤回 v0.6.19 对这些连接引入的 `operations/about` 查询路径。原生 SFTP 继续通过已认证的挂载会话读取容量、inode 的完整软硬配额。
- OpenSSH 挂载禁用终端分配、`LocalCommand` 和 `RemoteCommand`，避免终端配置干扰 SFTP 二进制数据流；服务器、私钥、跳板和指纹策略仍遵循原 SSH 配置。
- OpenSSH 负责指纹校验时，不再误报 rclone 未配置原生指纹校验。SFTP 启动失败时保留有限长度的 OpenSSH 原始错误，方便定位。
- 明确说明 SFTP 启动时的 `packet too long` 属于协议流异常，不是配额或文件大小超限。特定服务器的失败原因仍需结合 SSH 详情确认。
- 更新后请取消挂载并重新挂载，以使用新的连接参数和内置 rclone。
