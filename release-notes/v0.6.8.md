# SSH MountMate v0.6.8

Refine quota overage feedback, transfer refresh controls, and capacity labels.

- Capacity and inode quota bars keep the hard limit as their range and the neutral soft-limit divider.
  When usage exceeds a lower soft limit, only the over-limit segment gets sparse, low-contrast
  breathing dots. The selected accent color remains unchanged, and equal soft and hard limits have
  no divider.
- Keep the Transfer Center's manual refresh button alongside its automatic one-second refresh.
  The button keeps a stable appearance while a refresh is in flight; duplicate refresh requests
  are ignored.
- Display binary-style capacity suffixes as `KiB`, `MiB`, `GiB`, and `TiB` without changing the
  existing numeric scaling.

# SSH MountMate v0.6.8

细化配额超额提示、传输刷新控件和容量单位显示。

- 容量和 inode 配额进度继续以硬配额为范围，并保留中性的软配额细分割线。用量超过较低的软配额时，
  只在超额区间显示稀疏、低对比度的柔和呼吸小点；用户强调色保持不变，软硬配额相等时不显示分割线。
- 传输中心保留手动刷新按钮，同时继续每秒自动刷新。刷新进行中按钮外观保持稳定，重复刷新请求会被忽略。
- 容量单位显示为 `KiB`、`MiB`、`GiB`、`TiB`，现有数值换算保持不变。
